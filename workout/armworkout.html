💪 Visual Arm Workout Guide

Step-by-step instructions with start and finish positions

Perfect for beginners! Since you're already doing chest press at 40lbs, these exercises will help you build your arms using similar machine setups.
🎯 Start Here: Since you're new to these exercises, start with light weight (even just 10-20 lbs) to learn the movement first. You can always add more weight once you get the form down!
Cable Bicep Curls
Biceps
🔍 Find the: Cable machine (looks like a tower with pulleys and weights)
🟠 Starting Position
• Attach straight bar to BOTTOM pulley
• Stand facing the machine, feet shoulder-width apart
• Grab bar with palms facing UP (underhand grip)
• Arms fully extended down, elbows at your sides
• Step back 1-2 feet from machine
• Core tight, back straight
🟢 Finishing Position
• Curl bar up toward your chest
• Keep elbows glued to your sides (don't let them move)
• Squeeze biceps at the top
• Your hands should be near your shoulders
• Slowly lower back to start
• Don't let weight drop - control it down
3 Sets
12-15 Reps
60s Rest

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid:

  • Don't swing your body or use momentum
  • Don't let your elbows drift forward
  • Don't rush - slow and controlled wins
  • Don't use too much weight at first
Tricep Pushdowns
Triceps
🔍 Find the: Same cable machine, but use the TOP pulley this time
🟠 Starting Position
• Attach straight bar to TOP pulley
• Stand facing machine, feet shoulder-width apart
• Grab bar with palms facing DOWN (overhand grip)
• Upper arms against your sides, elbows bent 90°
• Bar should be around chest level
• Core engaged, slight lean forward
🟢 Finishing Position
• Push bar straight down until arms are fully extended
• Keep upper arms completely still
• Only your forearms should move
• Squeeze triceps at bottom
• Slowly let bar come back up to start
• Stop when elbows are at 90° again
3 Sets
10-12 Reps
60s Rest

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid:

  • Don't let your elbows flare out to the sides
  • Don't lean too far forward
  • Don't let the weight pull your arms up too high
  • Think: "Push the bar down to the floor"
Seated Row (Close Grip)
Biceps/Back
🔍 Find the: Seated row machine (looks like a bench with a cable in front)
🟠 Starting Position
• Sit on the bench, feet on footplates
• Grab close-grip handle (palms facing each other)
• Arms fully extended forward
• Slight bend in knees
• Sit up tall, chest out
• Feel a stretch in your back
🟢 Finishing Position
• Pull handle to your lower chest/upper stomach
• Squeeze shoulder blades together
• Keep elbows close to your body
• Feel your biceps and back working
• Slowly extend arms back to start
• Don't let your shoulders roll forward
3 Sets
10-12 Reps
90s Rest

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid:

  • Don't rock back and forth with your body
  • Don't pull too high (aim for stomach, not chest)
  • Don't let your shoulders round forward
  • Think: "Pull your elbows back behind you"
Bicep Curl Machine
Biceps
🔍 Find the: Bicep curl machine (has a slanted pad for your arms to rest on)
🟠 Starting Position
• Sit on the machine, adjust seat height
• Rest your upper arms on the angled pad
• Grab handles with palms facing up
• Arms should be almost fully extended
• Chest against the pad
• Feet flat on floor
🟢 Finishing Position
• Curl handles up toward your shoulders
• Keep your upper arms pressed against pad
• Squeeze biceps at the top
• Don't lift handles above shoulder level
• Slowly lower back to start
• Don't let weight slam down
2 Sets
12-15 Reps
60s Rest

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid:

  • Don't lift your upper arms off the pad
  • Don't arch your back
  • Don't curl too high (stop at shoulder level)
  • This machine makes it EASIER to do bicep curls correctly
